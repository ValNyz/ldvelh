**A solo narrative RPG where an AI writes your story in real time.**

Inspired by _Livres dont vous êtes le héros_ (Choose Your Own Adventure) and tabletop RPGs, LDVELH drops you into a procedurally generated space station where every choice shapes the world around you. No pre-written scripts — the AI narrates, remembers, and adapts.

## What is it?

You arrive on a space station with nothing but a few credits and questions. From there, the story is yours.

- **Talk to anyone** — NPCs have personalities, occupations, and opinions that evolve based on your actions
- **Go anywhere** — explore sectors, bars, workshops, docks — each with its own atmosphere
- **Make choices that matter** — the world remembers what you do, who you help, and who you cross
- **Watch stories unfold** — narrative arcs emerge, evolve, and resolve based on how you play

There are no levels, no combat system, no skill trees. Just you, a space station full of strangers, and the story you build by playing.

## How it works

Every turn, you type what you want to do — in plain French. The AI narrates what happens next, streaming the text to you in real time.

Behind the scenes, a knowledge graph tracks everything: characters you've met, places you've discovered, organizations, relationships, inventory, ongoing story arcs. The AI doesn't just generate text — it understands context and continuity.

## Features

- **Streaming narration** — text appears word by word, like a conversation
- **Living world** — NPCs move, relationships shift, arcs progress even when you're not looking
- **Persistent memory** — the game remembers across sessions, nothing is forgotten
- **Procedural world generation** — every new game creates a unique station with its own characters, factions, and tensions
- **Your own AI key** — bring your own Claude API key, or use the hosted service
- **French-language storytelling** — written in French, for the richness of literary narration

## Getting started

1. Go to **[ldvelh.up.railway.app](https://ldvelh.up.railway.app)**
2. Create an account
3. Start a new game — a world is generated for you
4. Type your first action and let the story begin
